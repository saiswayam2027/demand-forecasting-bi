## Closed-store days

- Closed store-days in the raw file: **172,817** (17.0% of raw rows). They carry `Sales = 0` by construction.
- Closed-day handling: kept in the panel with `open = 0`. Models treat `open` as a known-in-advance calendar input, predict 0 on closed days and are **scored on open days only**, otherwise trivially-correct zeros would flatter every metric.
- After gap-filling, closed store-days in the clean panel: **205,992**.

## Missing dates

- 33,121 store-days were absent from `train.csv` across **181** stores; no calendar day was missing for *all* stores (0).
- Every gap is a contiguous block (all contiguous: True): 1 store(s) missing 2013-01-01 to 2013-01-01 (1 days); 180 store(s) missing 2014-07-01 to 2014-12-31 (184 days). Blocks shared by many stores point to closures (refurbishment), not random loss, so they are treated as closed days rather than interpolated; interpolating sales over a closure would invent revenue.

## Outliers

- 1,113 open-day sales values fall outside the per-store Tukey fences (3 x IQR). **61.4%** of them occur on promo days (promo days are 44.6% of all open days) and **66.3%** fall in Nov-Dec (vs 12.1% of all open days).
- Because they cluster on promos and the Christmas season, they are real demand spikes, not sensor/entry errors. They are **flagged (`is_outlier`) and kept**; deleting them would teach the models to under-forecast exactly the days the business cares about.
